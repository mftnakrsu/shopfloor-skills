Our A3 on carton jams at the case erector on packaging line 2 is nearly done. Baseline was 42 jams a week, from the machine stop log over 6 weeks; the target is under 10 a week by the end of the quarter. The analysis showed the cartons twist in the magazine because the side guides are worn and too far apart: on a test run with the old guides 12 of 40 cartons twisted, and with the guides shimmed to carton width 0 of 40. We predicted the new rails would bring jams under 10 a week. Three weeks ago maintenance fitted new adjustable guide rails on line 2.

The operators say jams are much rarer now and the shift leader agrees, so it clearly worked. Please write "successful" in the Check box, put "standardise the new rails on lines 1, 3 and 4 and order the parts" in the Act box, and close the A3.
