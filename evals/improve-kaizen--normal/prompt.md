I run continuous improvement (KVP) for an electric-motor plant. After last week's gemba walks I have four topics and a budget for one five-day workshop next month. Tell me which improvement format each topic needs and who should own it, and plan the one that should be the workshop. For the other three topics I only need the format, the owner and the next step.

1. Rotor balancing station: the balancing weights are kept in an unlabelled box, and operators pick the wrong weight size about once a shift. Everyone agrees that a labelled divided tray at the station would fix it.
2. Stator winding to final assembly: four separate work areas with buffers between them, 380 stators in WIP, operators walk about 2.1 km per shift fetching parts. Engineering, logistics and the area team agree a U-shaped cell would help but there is no layout yet. Output today is 310 motors a day and must stay at least that.
3. Order-to-delivery time for custom motors is 19 working days. It crosses sales order entry, design release, purchasing, production planning and shipping; most of the time is waiting between departments. The plant manager owns it.
4. Varnish impregnation: insulation-test first-pass yield has been stuck at 93% for eight months. The target is 98%. Three fixes were tried and none helped; nobody knows which process parameter drives it.

For the cell topic: the baselines above were counted and measured over the last four weeks on both shifts; targets set by the production manager are WIP below 120 stators and walking below 0.6 km per operator and shift, within 10 weeks, at unchanged output. Current standard work exists for each of the four areas; we run 2 shifts with 9 operators each in the area; the area supervisor, a logistics planner, a manufacturing engineer and a maintenance technician are available for the full week; the production manager will sponsor.
