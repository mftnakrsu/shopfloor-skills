Short shots (incompletely filled parts) on our clip housings from injection moulding machine IM-4 have risen from 0.3% to 2.1% over the last 3 weeks (about 25,000 parts checked per week, found at the visual check at the end of the line). Facts so far: all short shots come from cavity 2 of the 4-cavity mould; cavities 1, 3 and 4 are fine. It happens on all three shifts at about the same rate. Machine IM-2 runs the same resin from the same dryer and has no short shots. The mould came back from a repair at the toolroom 3 weeks ago.

Our team's fishbone brainstorm:
- Man: new operator on night shift; operators don't care
- Machine: nozzle heater band weak; check ring worn
- Method: injection speed set too low
- Material: resin too wet (dryer); too much regrind
- Measurement: visual check misses small short shots
- Environment: hall is cold in the morning
- Also written on the board: "buy a new mould"

Turn this into cause hypotheses we can test, with a test plan and the order to test them.
