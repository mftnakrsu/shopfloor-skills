Our die change on press 7 takes 70 minutes and the target is 40. From our video, with the press stopped throughout:

1. Fetch the die from the store: 12 min
2. Unclamp and remove the old die: 10 min
3. Mount and clamp the new die, shim it to height: 15 min
4. Adjust stroke and feed, 4 trial hits: 13 min
5. Wait for quality to come and do the first-piece approval: 20 min

Our plan: the setter skips the first-piece approval and starts production straight away, and quality checks a part from the first box later in the shift. That saves 20 minutes. Is that OK?
