Our pump assembly line has 8 stations, each with a cycle time of 45 seconds, so our throughput time is 8 × 45 s = 6 minutes. That is what we put in the customer presentation. Between kitting and pack-out we hold about 1,200 pumps on average, and we ship 400 good pumps a day on one shift of 7.5 working hours. To cut the throughput time further, we plan to buy a faster press for station 3 that takes its cycle time from 45 to 35 seconds.

Please confirm the 6 minutes and that the press is the best way to cut our throughput time.
