We drew the future state for our gear-cover family. The customer takes 400 covers a day. The covers are stamped on a shared press that runs 12 part numbers; each changeover takes 2 hours, so today each cover part number comes round only about every 10 working days, and we hold 12 days of stamped covers in front of machining.

In the future-state map we replaced that pile with a kanban supermarket after the press sized at 1 day of demand (400 covers), with the press making whatever the kanban cards ask for. No other change is planned at the press. That cuts 11 days out of our production lead time, and we want to present the new lead time to management next week.

Please check the future state and confirm the 11-day saving.
