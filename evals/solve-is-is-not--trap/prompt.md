Our team finished the Is/Is-Not for short shots on a moulded cable clip. Please check it before we close it at the problem-solving meeting.

| | Is | Is not |
|---|---|---|
| What | Cable clip, short shot at the latch tip | Short shots on the base or the hinge |
| Where | Press 5 | Press 6, which runs the twin mould of the same clip |
| When | Since the night shift of 6 November, every shift | Before 6 November |
| Extent | 120 of 4,000 clips on press 5 since then | Press 6: 0 of 4,000 |

Changes:
- 4 November: new resin lot from our supplier. Both presses are fed from the same dryer, so both run this lot.
- 5 November: on press 5 the hot-runner controller for the latch-side zone was replaced.

Conclusion: root cause is the new resin lot. Action: send the lot back and ask the supplier for an 8D. Can we close it?
