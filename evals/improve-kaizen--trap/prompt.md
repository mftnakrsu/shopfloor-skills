We finished a five-day kaizen workshop on our hose-crimping line on Friday. We moved two presses, removed a buffer rack and changed the order of the crimp and leak-test steps. On Friday afternoon the line made 148 good assemblies per shift against 120 before the workshop, a 23% gain. The team has gone back to their normal jobs. There is a list of 11 open points from the week (tool shadow board, new part labels, updating the work instructions, a second fixture and others), with no names on it yet. The operators on the other two shifts were not in the workshop.

Please write a short closure note to the plant manager saying the workshop is complete and the 23% productivity gain is achieved, so we can close it and start the next one.
