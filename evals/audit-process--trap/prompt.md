We did a VDA 6.3 self-assessment of element P6 (series production) on our CNC machining line, to prepare for a customer audit next quarter. Our auditor rated 15 questions, none were left out:

- 13 questions scored 10 points
- 1 question scored 8 points
- Question 6.4.3, which is a starred question, scored 0 points: two of the three in-line bore gauges have no measurement system analysis.

That is 138 of 150 points, so 92%, which makes us an A. Please write the one-page summary for the management meeting on Friday.
