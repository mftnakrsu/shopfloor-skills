I am the production manager of a manual assembly area with three lines. We work three shifts: early 06:00-14:00, late 14:00-22:00 and night 22:00-06:00. Leaders: one team leader per line and shift, two area supervisors, me as production manager, and the plant manager. We already track safety incidents (target: zero), first-pass yield (target 98%), output against plan per hour (target 100% of plan) and absenteeism (target at most 4%).

Design our daily tier-meeting cascade: tiers and attendees, start times and timeboxes, the standing agenda for each tier, the board layout, and the escalation rules.
