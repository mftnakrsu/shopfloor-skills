I am the production manager of a manual assembly area with three lines. We work three shifts; the early shift starts at 06:00. Leaders: one team leader per line and shift, two area supervisors, me as production manager, and the plant manager. We already track safety incidents, first-pass yield, output against plan per hour, and absenteeism.

Design our daily tier-meeting cascade: tiers and attendees, start times and timeboxes, the standing agenda for each tier, the board layout, and the escalation rules.
