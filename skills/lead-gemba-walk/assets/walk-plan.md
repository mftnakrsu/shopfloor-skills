## Questions

Ask in the order what → why → what if → why not. Ask the people doing the work.

1. What is the process here?
2. How can you tell it is working?
3. What do you do when it is not working?

## Rules for the walkers

- Watch at least one full work cycle at each stop before asking.
- Record facts; no names in the log; no judgement of people.
- No fixes or orders on the spot; contain only safety and product-quality risks.
- Close the walk with a reflection: what did we see, what did we learn?
