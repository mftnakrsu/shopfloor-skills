# 8D report

How to write the 8D report, and what customers typically expect in format and timing.

## Writing rules

1. Facts, not opinions: numbers with units, dates, lot numbers, photos, measured values.
2. One section per discipline, D0 to D8, in order, even when one is short. Write "not needed, because …" rather than leaving a section empty.
3. Every action has an owner (a role or a name the user gives), a due date and a status.
4. Every claim of "done" or "effective" points to an attachment: a record, a photo, a data table.
5. D2 holds no cause and no solution.
6. Name the root causes separately: occurrence, escape and, where found, systemic. Each has its own action line.
7. Mark open items as open. An 8D with open items is an interim report, not a closed one.
8. Use the customer's form or portal where they prescribe one (IATF 16949 10.2.3); keep the same content.
9. A statement is not evidence of a change. Attach the revised instruction, PFMEA or control plan (Creation Technologies).

## Interim and final reports

- **Interim:** D0 to D3 complete, D4 to D8 as a plan with owners and dates. Send it when the customer's first deadline falls.
- **Root-cause update:** D4 and D5 with verification evidence.
- **Final:** D6 validation, D7 document revisions and read-across, D8 closure.

## Response times are customer-specific

There is no general rule. Ask for the customer's requirement and use it. Published examples, each one customer-specific:

| Source | Containment | Root cause and action plan | Full response or closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| An electronics contract manufacturer's supplier training | 3 business days | 10 business days | 30 calendar days; closed only when the customer signs off |
| A test-equipment maker's supplier guideline | — | — | response to the corrective action request within 14 days |
| A blog post on supplier quality, critical issues | 4 hours, first response within 24 hours | — | — |
| The same blog post, high priority | 48 hours | — | 8D within 14 days |

The same blog post gives validation windows of about 90 days or 10 production lots as examples; set yours by local policy or the customer.
