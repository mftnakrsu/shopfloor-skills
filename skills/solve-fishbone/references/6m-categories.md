# 6M categories

The default bones, what belongs on each, and questions that help a team fill them. Ishikawa offered the Ms as generic labels and encouraged naming categories to suit the process (ASQ). Common variants are 5M (no environment) and 8M (adding management, maintenance and money or mission) (Wikipedia).

## Bones and prompt questions

| Bone | Covers | Prompt questions |
|---|---|---|
| Man (people, Mensch) | Skills, training and release, workload, handovers, communication | Is the person trained and released for this task? Was anyone new, rushed or interrupted? Does the instruction match what is actually done? What in the process lets a person get it wrong? |
| Machine (Maschine) | Equipment, tools, fixtures, programs, wear, maintenance state | Which machine, tool, nest or spindle makes the bad parts? What was repaired, changed or adjusted? Is anything worn, loose, blocked, leaking or out of its set range? |
| Method (Methode) | Sequence, settings, parameters, work instructions, handling, transport | Which parameters are set, and are they kept? Did a program, recipe or sequence change? Is the method the same on every shift and station? |
| Material (Material) | Raw material, purchased parts, consumables, lots, storage | Which lots, suppliers or batches are involved? Is the material inside its specification and its storage or shelf-life limits? Was there a change of supplier or lot? |
| Measurement (Messung) | Gauges, checks, test programs, sampling, counting rules | Can the check see the defect reliably? Did the gauge, program, limit or person doing the check change? Could the rise be in the counting rather than in the parts? |
| Environment (Mitwelt, milieu, mother nature) | Temperature, humidity, dust, light, vibration, cleanliness | Does the effect follow the time of day, the season or the weather? Is the affected area different from the unaffected one in climate, dust or light? |

## Using the bones

- Measurement first: if the count comes from a new or changed check, test the check before the process. A false rise or a missed defect shows up on this bone, not on the others.
- Place each cause on one bone only. If it fits two, put it where the test will be done.
- A bone with no entries is a prompt to ask, not proof that nothing is there.
- A cause about a person is written as the process condition that allows the error. "Operator error" is not accepted as a root cause (Creation Technologies).
- Split a crowded bone (machine into tool and machine; material into raw material and purchased parts) instead of adding a seventh vague one.
