# Kaizen event

How to charter, prepare, run and follow up a kaizen event (workshop, blitz). LEI gives "commonly five days"; the day plan below is a practitioner synthesis, not one source's rule.

## Charter (before preparation starts)

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Gap | Metric, measured baseline (value, method, period, shifts), target, date |
| Scope | First and last step or station; what is out of scope |
| Limits | What must not get worse: output, quality, safety, cost cap |
| Sponsor | The manager who removes obstacles and attends the report-out |
| Team leader | Runs the week; not the sponsor |
| Team | Operators of the area from every shift where possible, the area supervisor, and the functions the change needs (engineering, logistics, maintenance, quality) |
| Dates | Event week, report-out, 30/60/90 checks |

Management support and goal difficulty were among the most consistent predictors of event outcomes in a field study of 51 event teams (Farris). Set a target that stretches the team, and get the sponsor's time booked before the week.

## Preparation (rule of thumb: start 2 to 4 weeks before)

1. Measure the baseline with the method that will be used afterwards.
2. Collect the current standard work, layout and recent data for the area.
3. Book the team for the full week; release operators from production with cover.
4. Line up maintenance, facilities and safety support for moves and tests.
5. Agree how output is protected during the week (stock built ahead, or changes outside shift hours).

## The week (practitioner synthesis)

| Day | Work | Leaves the day with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short training on the method; go to the area; observe and time the current state | Current-state facts against the current standard |
| 2 | Analyse waste and causes; design the future state | Agreed target design and test plan |
| 3 | Build and try (move, mock up, run a trial) | First trial results |
| 4 | Adjust, run again, write the new standard work and visual standards | Draft standard, trained trial operators |
| 5 | Run on the new standard; report-out to the sponsor | Trial result, open-action list with owners and dates, sustain plan |

- The trial result on day 5 is not the verified gain. It shows that the design works once; the gain is verified over the verification period after the event.
- Never leave the area worse than on Monday: the line must run on Monday of the next week.

## Report-out

Show the gap, what changed, the trial result against the target, the new standard, the open actions with owners and dates, and the 30/60/90 dates. The sponsor accepts the open-action list and the sustain plan.

## After the event

- Post-event performance review, acceptance of the changes, and learning and stewardship in the work area predicted results 9 to 18 months after the event (Glover).
- Good attitudes at the end of a successful event do not by themselves turn into sustained improvement (Doolen).
- So the follow-up is part of the event: the 30/60/90 sustain plan starts at the report-out.
