Plans, runs and follows up gemba walks (Gemba-Rundgang) on the shop floor in three modes: the process walk (standard work, waste, flow), the management-system walk (boards, reds, escalations, leader standard work) and the value-stream walk (the end-to-end current state). An optional layered process audit (LPA) segment can be added to a walk.

## Three phases

The skill picks the phase from your request.

| Phase | You give | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | The purpose, the area and who walks, and the time available | A walk plan: type, route along the flow, timed segments, open questions, what to look for, and LPA items if asked |
| Run or write up | Your observations from the walk | An observation log stated as process facts, contained risks first, the top findings, owners and due dates, and who tells the team |
| Follow up | The log with its status and the open items | Overdue findings, verification at the gemba, and the walk metrics |

A plan needs the purpose, the area and who walks; without them the skill asks and stops. It never invents a route, stations, problems, KPI values or LPA control points for your area.

## Rules it applies

- Observe; do not fix or give orders on the spot, except to contain a safety or quality risk, or to correct an LPA No within the walker's authority.
- No blame: a standard not followed or people standing idle is first a question about the process, and the log names stations and roles, not people.
- A recurring disturbance handled locally is still a finding.
- The timed segments add up to no more than the time available.
- Every finding has an owner, a due date, a route and a verification step, and the team hears back.
- A finding closes only when someone has seen at the gemba that the action works; an LPA No corrected during the audit still counts as a No.

## Where the work goes next

Actions with a known cause go to the board in [`lead-daily-routine`](/skills/lead-daily-routine/), and findings that need an improvement format go to [`improve-kaizen`](/skills/improve-kaizen/). Customer, safety or repeat problems go to [`solve-8d`](/skills/solve-8d/). Value-stream facts go to [`improve-vsm`](/skills/improve-vsm/), equipment losses to [`measure-oee`](/skills/measure-oee/), and waiting and piles of WIP to [`improve-tpt`](/skills/improve-tpt/) or [`improve-kanban-pull`](/skills/improve-kanban-pull/).

## What is in the folder

- `SKILL.md`: the procedure and the checks.
- `references/`: walk design, the layered audit and follow-up, each with its sources.
- `assets/`: a walk plan, an observation log and an LPA checklist.
