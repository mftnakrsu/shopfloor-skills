Chooses and runs the right continuous-improvement (KVP) format for a shop-floor problem or idea: a point fix, a kaizen event (workshop, blitz), system kaizen across departments, or a kata or A3 when the way to the target is unknown. Every result ends in a new standard, a check that the standard is used, and a sustain plan.

## What you give and what you get

| You give | You get |
|---|---|
| One or more topics, each with its metric, measured baseline and target | The format per topic with a one-line reason, an owner and the next step |
| For an event: scope, the current standard, sponsor, team and constraints | A charter, the five-day plan, the new standard, its check (who, how, how often) and a 30/60/90-day sustain plan |
| A result to close or a gain to report | Whether it can be closed, what is still missing, and the check dates |

Without the problem, the baseline or the target, the skill asks for them in one short list and stops. It never writes a target, baseline or saving onto a charter that you did not give.

## Rules it applies

- Kaizen starts from a current standard; with none, writing today's best method comes first.
- The format follows the gap: an event on a problem with an unknown cause ends in guesses; a point fix on a flow problem moves it elsewhere.
- The gain is measured with the baseline method over every shift and the normal mix, not on the last day of the event.
- Every result names the new standard, its owner and the training of all shifts, and a check of its use.
- Every open action has one owner and a due date.
- A topic closes when the 90-day check passes, not at the report-out.

Machine losses go to [`measure-oee`](/skills/measure-oee/), and whether a gain is real is checked with [`measure-spc`](/skills/measure-spc/).
