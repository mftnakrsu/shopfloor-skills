The logo is a yamazumi chart of a balanced line. Three stations split their work into 1, 2 and 3 blocks, and every stack ends level just under one takt line.

![The shopfloor-skills horizontal lockup](../../../assets/logo-light.svg)

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Graphite | `#1F2428` | Stacks and text on light backgrounds; the icon tile |
| Line Green | `#2E8A55` | The takt line on light backgrounds |
| Line Green on dark | `#3FA56A` | The takt line on dark backgrounds |
| Chalk | `#F1F4F2` | Stacks and text on dark backgrounds |

The typeface is Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. In the logo files the wordmark is converted to paths. This site loads the font from its own files; the licence is at [/fonts/OFL-BarlowSemiCondensed.txt](/fonts/OFL-BarlowSemiCondensed.txt).

The full guidelines, with clear space, minimum sizes and don'ts, are in [assets/brand/BRAND.md](https://github.com/mftnakrsu/shopfloor-skills/blob/main/assets/brand/BRAND.md).
